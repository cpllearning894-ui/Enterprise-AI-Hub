
ENTERPRISE AI HUB - IT POLICY

Password Reset:
Employees who forget their passwords
must contact the IT Help Desk.

Software Installation:
Employees must obtain IT approval
before installing software on company computers.

Technical Support:
The IT Help Desk is available Monday to Friday,
from 8:00 AM to 5:00 PM.

Company Devices:
Employees must report lost or damaged
company laptops to the IT department.

AI Tools Policy:

Employees must obtain IT department approval before using new AI tools with company data.
