
ENTERPRISE AI HUB - COMPANY POLICY

Company Name: Enterprise AI Hub Demo Company

Working Hours:
Employees work from Monday to Friday,
8:00 AM to 5:00 PM.

Annual Leave:
Full-time employees receive 18 days of
paid annual leave per year.

Remote Work:
Employees may work remotely up to
2 days per week with manager approval.

IT Support:
Employees should contact the IT department
for technical problems.

Data Security:
Employees must not share passwords,
API keys, or confidential company data
with unauthorized people.
